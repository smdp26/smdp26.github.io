I made an album :)

October 22, 2026

#Music #Production

I am releasing my album “across the paddy fields” today and I wanted to write a blog post about the process of making the album. I am releasing it on YouTube and Bandcamp. I have linked both below, feel free to take a listen before (while?) reading the post :)

Around January of 2025, I decided I want to write an album. I took the challenge that I will (try to) write a full album’s worth of music within a year. I didn’t think I would actually succeed but I was already happy with what I had around August.

I would describe the style of music on the album as ambient/ambient-adjacent. I love ambient music and have been trying to make ambient music for quite some time now; basically since I discovered Boards of Canada. I still think Dayvan Cowboy and Roygbiv are two of the best songs ever made. Later on I listened to more ambient music and discovered many new artists.

What drew me to ambient music is the depth in the textures; the textures are interesting enough to keep the listener’s attention without relying on melody, harmony or rhythm.

The first time I was happy with ambient music I made was two audio palindromes I made for a jam on itch.io (these songs sound the same played both forwards and backwards).

When I started writing songs for the album, I didn’t have any particular style in mind. In fact, initially I was going for a more downtempo style. But while making some songs in that style, I got really annoyed with the drum samples I was using I am not really sure why 😅. I got annoyed to the point that I thought I will just try to make some songs without using any drums at all. That’s when I started writing some ambient songs. In particular, I wrote satoshi during this time. Also both wake up,wake up and across the paddy fields were actually made by resampling one of the downtempo songs I thought was not good enough to be released on its own.

There are many different styles and moods in ambient itself. An album by Sunn o))) sounds very different from one by Hiroshi Yoshimura. Again, I didn’t preemptively decide any style. I essentially parallelly made songs in two styles: a calmer, nostalgic style which ended up on the final album and a different darker, grungier style. I made 35 songs in total. I chose the best songs that made sense stylistically as one full album. For the calmer songs I intentionally used a lot sounds I liked as a child. I also thought what I liked as a child when making those songs and it made thematic sense to make an album out of those. These songs had a sense of playfulness while being nostalgic which I really liked. The sounds of train in the start of the album is where the name of the album comes from. I used to go on long train journeys and stare out of the window looking at long stretches of paddy fields.

I wrote a few more songs to round out the transition in the middle and the ending and I was already done by around October of 2025. Then, partly due to being busy and partly due to procrastination, it took me a year to finally release the album. After finishing the songs, I sent the album to few of my friends to see what they thought. They liked it, even the much weirder songs in the first part of the album. For the album cover I tried a few things and made a few composite images but I found the picture “Štubnianske Teplice” by Mikuláš Galanda and it fit the album perfectly. I deliberated whether I should put the album on the streaming services or just YouTube and Bandcamp but after some research I realised there are no good distributors and I was not willing to subscribe to a service that costs non-zero amount of dollars every month and gives me an expected return of zero dollars 😅.
